## Report Open Conduit's own defects (`introspection.report-issue`)

When the fix for something you hit belongs to Open Conduit itself (a toolbelt operation that fails or misbehaves, a playbook that is wrong, a run that went astray), report it, as well as any problem note, with `kind` one of `env`, `docs`, `tooling`, `service`, `runtime` or `other`, a one-line `title` and a `body` saying what you tried, the exact error, your workaround and where the fix belongs. Report each defect once per session. A refusal means reporting isn't enabled here: carry on.
